Frequently asked questions

Are the prices final?

You see one HomeBasket price for each item. We confirm availability and current prices before accepting your order. If anything changes, we send you a final quotation to approve first.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your basket, enter your delivery details at checkout and send the order to us on WhatsApp. Our team replies to confirm.

How much is delivery?

The delivery fee depends on your area and is shown at checkout. If your area is not listed, we will confirm the fee with you.

What if an item is unavailable?

We tell you before your order is confirmed and suggest an alternative where possible. Nothing is changed without your approval.

Can businesses order in bulk?

Yes. Send us your supply list through the business quotation page and we will prepare a quotation.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared by our team when your order is confirmed.
